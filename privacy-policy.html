Privacy Policy

App: PuffRoast - Quit Vape & Smoke (Bundle ID: us.puffroast.quitlog) · Last Updated: November 23, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection & 100% On-Device Architecture

PuffRoast is built as a strictly offline, zero-account habit companion. All craving classification, 4-7-8 breathing SOS sessions, Treat Vault calculations, and 9:16 victory cards run completely on your iPhone or iPad. We do not collect, transmit, rent, sell, or analyze any personal data. Specifically, PuffRoast never accesses or collects:

  • Names, email addresses, account logins, passwords, or phone numbers
  • Your craving logs, slip-up check-ins, wishlist items, or smoke-free streaks
  • Camera, microphone, location, contacts, HealthKit, or photo library permissions
  • Cloud AI APIs, third-party analytics SDKs, crash telemetry, or advertising IDs (IDFA)

2. Local Sandbox Storage & Privacy-Scrubbed Exports

Your baseline settings, craving logs, and wishlist goals are saved strictly inside your device's local app sandbox (`PuffRoastVault.json`). Uninstalling PuffRoast removes all local files. When you tap "9:16 Card" or "Share Local JSON Backup," PuffRoast strips potential emails, phone numbers, and handles from share posters and hands the export solely to the native iOS Share Sheet for your chosen destination.

3. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit 2)

Optional PuffRoast Pro plans (Lifetime, Annual with 3-day free trial, and Monthly) and "Restore Purchases" requests are handled exclusively by Apple Inc. through the App Store and StoreKit 2. The developer never sees your payment card, billing address, or Apple ID.

4. Target Regions & Age Rating (17+)

PuffRoast is distributed exclusively in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia (excluding the EU-27 and Mainland China) and carries a 17+ App Store rating due to adult smoking/vaping habit references. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone of any age because zero personal data leaves your device.
