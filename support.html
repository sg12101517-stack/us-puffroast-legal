Thank you for using PuffRoast - Quit Vape & Smoke (Bundle ID: us.puffroast.quitlog). Below are answers to common questions and direct support contact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is included Free vs. PuffRoast Pro?

Free includes the live smoke-free timer, unlimited 60s 4-7-8 Craving SOS sessions, Grumpy Lungs & Kind Coach personas, 1 Treat Wishlist goal, 1 daily 9:16 victory share card, and the first 3 Recovery milestones. PuffRoast Pro unlocks Frugal Butler & Sassy Bestie, Extra Crispy spice mode, unlimited goals, 3 mint themes, unlimited 9:16 posters, and all 7 Recovery milestones.

Q2. Where do the Recovery Timeline health milestones come from?

All seven educational milestones (20 min, 12 hrs, 24 hrs, 72 hrs, 2 weeks, 1 month, 1 year) summarize publicly published tobacco cessation guidance from the CDC and WHO. PuffRoast is not a medical device or clinical treatment program.

Q3. Does PuffRoast delete my Treat Jar if I log a slip-up?

No. PuffRoast follows a gentle "Slip ≠ Failure" flow. When you confess a puff, your overall habit motivation journey remains visible, and you choose whether to keep going or reset only the active timer.

Q4. How do I restore my Pro unlock on another iPhone or iPad?

Sign in with the same Apple ID used for your purchase, open the Deck tab in PuffRoast, and tap "Restore Purchases."
